About SoundLoop

SoundLoop is a small three-page website project built around a featured audio file. Its goal is to make listening simple and to show how pages can connect through a consistent navigation menu.

Website goals

  • Provide a clear home page that introduces the site.
  • Offer an audio page with visitor-controlled playback.
  • Explain the project and show a simple information table.

Page structure

Every page links to Home, Listen, and About so visitors can move around the site easily.
