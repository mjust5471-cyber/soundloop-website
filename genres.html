Music genres

Music comes in many styles. Here are a few popular genres and what makes each one distinct.

Pop

Catchy melodies, memorable hooks, and a wide range of influences.

Rock

Often built around guitars, bass, drums, and strong rhythms.

Hip-Hop

Rhythmic vocals and beats, with creativity in flow and production.

Jazz

Expressive improvisation, syncopated rhythms, and rich harmonies.

Electronic

Synthesizers, programmed beats, and sound design.

Classical

Orchestral and instrumental traditions with varied forms and textures.
